## 🌟 Project Overview

The **Sales Performance Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI project designed to give a **360° view of your sales data**. It helps businesses monitor key metrics, uncover trends, and make **data-driven decisions** with ease.

---

## 🎯 Key Features

* **Interactive Analytics** – Explore sales by product categories, regions, and time periods.  
* **Critical KPIs** – Track revenue, profit margin, sales growth, and more.  
* **Drill-Down Insights** – Examine detailed data by products, sales reps, or customer segments.  
* **Dynamic Filtering** – Apply slicers and filters to focus on specific datasets.  
* **Responsive Layout** – Optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.
